---
title: Get started
description: From nothing to a running application on the EGRADX SDK, in Java, Node or Python.
---

<Steps>
<Step>

### Understand the idea

Five minutes on what the SDK is and what it expects of your application: [Concepts](/docs/get-started/concepts).

</Step>
<Step>

### Get access to the packages

The packages are on GitHub Packages, which needs a token even to read them: [Access to the packages](/docs/get-started/access).

</Step>
<Step>

### Run a first application

Follow the quickstart for your language: [Java](/docs/get-started/quickstart-java), [Node](/docs/get-started/quickstart-node) or [Python](/docs/get-started/quickstart-python). Each ends with you signed in, calling the SDK's own endpoints. Or generate a complete project, with a frontend if you like, in [the Initializr](/start).

</Step>
<Step>

### Add your first endpoint

A protected, audited endpoint of your own, and how OIM does the same: [Your first endpoint](/docs/get-started/first-endpoint).

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout title="Using an AI assistant?">
  Download the [AI context pack](/docs/ai-assistants) into your project first. Your assistant then knows what the SDK already does.
</Callout>
