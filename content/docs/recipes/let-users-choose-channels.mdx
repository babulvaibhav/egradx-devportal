---
title: Let users choose their notification channels
description: People add and verify their addresses (phone, Slack, WhatsApp), and choose which messages they get where.
---

## 1. Make the message optional

Only types declared `userCanOptOut()` appear on people's settings: see [add a notification type](/docs/recipes/add-a-notification-type). Account messages, such as password reset, can never be turned off.

## 2. Add the settings screen

The ready screen does both halves: addresses verified by code, and on/off per message and channel.

```sh
npx shadcn@latest add https://<this portal>/r/notification-settings.json
```

Or build your own on the hooks from `@babulvaibhav/egradx-react`:

| Hook | Gives |
|---|---|
| `useContacts()` | `data.contacts` and `data.options`; `add`, `remove`, `makePrimary`, `startVerification`, `confirmVerification` |
| `useNotificationPreferences()` | `data.options` (the types and channels people may choose from), `data.preferences`; `save(preferences)` |

`save` replaces every stored preference. Send back the ones your form doesn't show, or they are lost.

## How the SDK uses them

- A user receives on a channel only at an address they have **verified** with a code. Email is the exception: it falls back to the address they sign in with.
- A preference turned off is honoured on every send to that user. Sends to raw addresses (`toAddresses`) have no preferences.
- Which channels people can add is set by `profile.channels` (Java `egradx.auth.profile.channels`; default email, sms, whatsapp, slack).

## Over HTTP

| Endpoint | |
|---|---|
| `/egradx/auth/my/profile/contacts/**` | a person's addresses, and verifying them |
| `GET /egradx/notifications/preference-options` | what can be chosen |
| `GET`, `PUT /egradx/auth/my/profile/preferences` | the choices |
