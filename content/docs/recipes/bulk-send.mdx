## What happens

1. `send` writes **one row** and returns at once with a `bulkSendId`, so a large organization doesn't make the request slow.
2. In the background, the members are expanded **200 at a time** into deliveries, each to that person's verified address on the channel (for email, the address they sign in with).
3. Each delivery is sent, retried and logged like any other. People who turned the type off are skipped.

Follow it with `GET /egradx/notifications/admin/bulk-sends/{id}`, which shows its status and its counts.

## Limits

- **At most 1000 recipients** by default: Java `egradx.notification.bulk.max-recipients`, Node `bulk: { maxRecipients }`, Python `bulk_max_recipients`. A larger organization is refused rather than cut short.
- Ready-made content (`EgradxMessageContent.of(subject, body)`) needs no template. A notification type with templates works too, and is translated per person.
- To reach **everyone on the platform**, the platform's administrators use `/egradx/notifications/admin/platform/bulk-sends`.

## In the app as well

A bulk send is a message on a channel. For the bell, publish an in-app notice too: `publishToOrganization` reaches every member in one row.
