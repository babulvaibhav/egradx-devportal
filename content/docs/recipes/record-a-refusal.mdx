---
title: Record a refused or failed attempt
description: Keep a record of what someone tried and was refused — written on its own, so it stays when the work around it rolls back.
---

A successful change is recorded with the change. A refused or failed attempt is different: the work usually rolls back, but the attempt must stay on record. Mark it `denied(reason)` or `failure(reason)`, and the SDK writes it in a transaction of its own.

| | Outcome | Written |
|---|---|---|
| (neither) | `SUCCESS` | with your transaction: kept only if it commits |
| `.denied(code)` | `DENIED`: a rule said no | on its own: kept even on rollback |
| `.failure(code)` | `FAILURE`: it went wrong | on its own: kept even on rollback |

Record the refusal, then throw as usual (for example an `AuthorizationException`), so the caller gets the error envelope.

## Repeats are throttled

Identical refusals in a burst (a password guessed many times) are kept once a minute, so an attacker can't flood the trail. `egradx.audit.suppressed` counts the ones dropped.

## The SDK's own

The SDK already records its own refusals: failed sign-ins, refused role changes and so on. [Audit actions](/docs/reference/audit-actions) lists them.
