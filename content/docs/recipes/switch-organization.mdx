Someone can belong to several organizations. A session acts in one of them at a time, and switching issues new tokens.

## At sign-in

Signing in as someone with several organizations fails with `ORG_SELECTION_REQUIRED`. Its `details.organizations` lists them; sign in again naming the chosen `organizationId`:

## Later, from a menu

<RepoSnippet file="apps/egradx-devportal/examples/web-vite/src/recipes/OrganizationSwitcher.tsx" region="switch-organization" title="frontend/src/recipes/OrganizationSwitcher.tsx" />

`switchOrganization(id)` calls `POST /egradx/auth/my/organizations/{id}/switch`, replaces the tokens with those of a new session in the other organization, and ends the session left: its refresh token stops working at once.

## Leave nothing behind

- **The SDK's hooks** (`useInbox`, `useAuditLog`, the stores) start over for the new organization on their own.
- **Your own caches** don't. Key them by `sessionScope(session)` from `@babulvaibhav/egradx-client`, which changes when the person or the organization changes. With TanStack Query, put it in every query key, or clear the cache when it changes.
- **The live stream** reopens for the new organization.

OIM's browser tests switch organization and check that nothing of the previous one is still shown.

## With Next.js

The same component works: the backend-for-frontend switches, and keeps the new tokens in its cookies.
