## 1. Record it where the change is made

Load the current state, apply the change, then record both states, inside the same transaction:

## 2. Name the action

`category.entity.verb`, lower case, past tense: `notes.note.updated`. The category is the first word, and the audit screen filters on it. Describe your actions once so the screen shows words people understand: see [the catalog](/docs/guides/audit#your-actions-on-the-audit-screen).

## What it records

<RecordedRun name="audit" steps={['update', 'update-record']} />

- `changedFields` lists the top-level fields that differ. It is worked out before secrets are redacted, so a changed password shows as changed without its value.
- Keys such as `password`, `token`, `secret` and `otp` are redacted wherever they appear in a state.
- The actor, the organization, the request id and the trace id are filled in from the request.

## The thing's history

`GET /egradx/audit/timeline?entityType=note&entityId=…` gives one note's records, newest first. In React, `useAuditTimeline('note', id)`, or the copy-in `AuditHistoryButton`.
