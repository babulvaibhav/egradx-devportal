### Is there an EGRADX server to run?

No. The SDK is libraries that run inside your application, on your PostgreSQL database. Sign-in, audit and notifications are endpoints and tables of your application.

### Can a Java service and a Node service share users?

Yes. Point them at the same database, keys folder, passphrase and issuer. They share the tables, and accept each other's tokens.

### Do I need RabbitMQ or Kafka?

Not to start. Events are in-process by default. Add a broker when a second service must handle your events ([move to RabbitMQ](/docs/recipes/move-to-rabbitmq)). Notifications never need one.

### Can I use MySQL?

No: PostgreSQL only, for now.

### Can I use my ORM?

Yes, for your own tables: JPA, Prisma, SQLAlchemy. The SDK's tables are created by its own migrations; don't map or alter them. In Node, run your work in `egradxTransaction` so the audit records and events join it.

### Where do I check permissions: roles or authorities?

Authorities, always (`note.write`). Roles belong to each organization's administrators and can change.

### Why does another organization's record give 404, not 403?

So ids can't be probed. An id from another organization looks exactly like one that doesn't exist.

### How do I make someone an administrator of the whole platform?

The platform organization's `Admin` role. The `platform-admin` account is created on the first start.

### Does the SDK send emails synchronously?

No. Sends are queued in your transaction and delivered after the commit by a dispatcher inside your application, with retries.

### What does the browser need?

`@babulvaibhav/egradx-client`, plus `@babulvaibhav/egradx-react` for React, or `@babulvaibhav/egradx-next` for a backend-for-frontend in Next.js. [The Initializr](/start) generates either.

### The packages need a token even to install?

Yes, while they are private on GitHub Packages: see [access](/docs/get-started/access).

### Where do I report a problem?

Every page has a link at the bottom to open an issue about that page.
